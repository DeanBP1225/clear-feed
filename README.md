# Clear Feed – Hide Promoted Posts

A free browser extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge. It hides sponsored posts, "Suggested for you" posts and the sponsored box in the right column of your Facebook feed.

- Runs entirely in your browser. It collects no data and makes no network requests.
