Clear Feed – Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Clear Feed is a browser extension that hides sponsored and suggested posts on Facebook pages.

  • Data we collect: none. Clear Feed does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history or page content.
  • Data stored on your device: your settings (on/off, hide "Suggested for you", hide the sidebar sponsored box, debug logging) and a count of hidden sponsored posts. These are kept in your browser's extension storage and never leave your device. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • Network activity: Clear Feed makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking code. The optional "Support (PayPal)" link only opens PayPal in a new tab when you click it.
  • Diagnostic report: if you click "Copy diagnostic report", a description of the posts on screen is copied to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere. You decide whether to share it.
  • Third parties: none.
  • Changes: if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here and described in the extension's update notes.
